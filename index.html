Advanced training options

Progress gradually

Tempo reps

Lower the weight for 3 seconds, pause briefly, then lift smoothly. Use on one or two exercises.

Supersets

Pair non-competing moves, such as curls + triceps extensions. Rest after both exercises.

Progressive overload

When all sets reach the top of the rep range with clean form, add a small amount of weight.

Keep 1–3 good reps in reserve. Avoid drop sets or training to failure on every set; recovery matters.
